What I need before I build

A website goes faster when these four things are clear on day one.

1. Pages. Home, work, about, contact, and anything else that must exist at launch. If a page can wait, say so.

2. Examples. Two or three sites you like, and one you do not. I use that to match layout and tone. I will not copy another person’s site.

3. Deadline. A real date. If photos or copy are not ready, the date moves.

4. Who updates it. If you want to change text and photos yourself, we build admin. If not, I handle updates.

Send those in the contact form. I reply within 24 hours on weekdays.
