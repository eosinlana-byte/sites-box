Why the contact form matters

Pretty pages do not pay you. A message in your inbox does.

Keep the form short: name, email, message. Extra dropdowns make people leave.

Put the form on the first visit path. Home should point to it. Contact should not be buried.

Show that you reply. “I reply within 24 hours” is enough. Then actually reply.

On Jackson, Okolibooks, and Mirah, the form is the point of the site, not a leftover footer box.
