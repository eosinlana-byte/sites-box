CHECKLIST

10 points so your site can get more enquiries

Use this before we start, or on a site you already have.

1. Write one sentence for what you do.

2. Write who it is for.

3. List the pages you need on day one. Home, work, about, contact is enough for many people.

4. Pick two sites you like and one you do not. Say why in a line each.

5. Get your logo and photos in one folder. Real photos beat stock.

6. Decide the one action: send a message, book a time, or email you.

7. Put that action on the first screen, not only in the footer.

8. Put your email on the contact page. People should not hunt for it.

9. Say how fast you reply. “Within 24 hours” is enough.

10. Decide who will change text and photos after launch. You, or me.
