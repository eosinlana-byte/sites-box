ABOUT SMITH MICHAEL

I’m Smith Michael, a website designer and developer who helps businesses, individuals, and brands build their online presence.

My work includes business websites, personal portfolios, landing pages, creative studio websites, and custom web applications. I also build features such as contact forms, admin dashboards, and content management tools to make websites easier to manage.

Whether you need a new website, want to improve an existing one, or need ongoing updates, I’m available to help.
